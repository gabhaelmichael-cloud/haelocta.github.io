Human-Computer Interaction

Project: The EV Edit (Proton e.MAS Analysis)

An expert evaluation of the Proton e.MAS website usability based on Nielsen's 10 Heuristics.

  • Visibility of System Status: Assessing how the site communicates progress during the booking process.
  • Recognition vs. Recall: Analyzing the navigation menu to reduce user cognitive load.
  • User Control and Freedom: Reviewing "Emergency Exits" for users when making errors.
  • Consistency and Standards: Ensuring design patterns match industry expectations.
