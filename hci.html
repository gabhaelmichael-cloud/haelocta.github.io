Smart EV Dashboard Prototype

Project Scope & Context

Designing a homegrown Electric Vehicle (EV) dashboard for the Sarawak Ministry of Transport (MOT)[cite: 2].

  • Sarawak Context: Tailored for intense sun glare, heavy rain, and Pan Borneo long-distance travel[cite: 2].
  • Integrated Modules: Includes Energy & Charging, Climate Control, and Navigation & Range systems[cite: 2].
  • Design Strategy: Critical response to touchscreen-only controls, prioritizing driver safety and glanceable interaction[cite: 2].

HCI Requirements & Theory

  • Motor-Control: Precise application of Fitts' Law for touch target sizing[cite: 2].
  • Cognitive Principles: Managing attention and reducing mental workload during travel[cite: 2].
  • Glanceability: Ensuring information is distinguishable within 2-3 steps of interaction[cite: 2].

Team Milestones

  • User Research: Affinity and Persona diagrams based on EV/hybrid user interviews[cite: 2].
  • Architecture: Low-Fi wireframes and high-contrast Hi-Fi Figma prototypes[cite: 2].
  • Validation: Think-aloud testing and System Usability Scale (SUS) reporting[cite: 2].
