System Modeling Frameworks

Mapping technical infrastructure to organizational needs through structured diagrams.

  • Context Diagrams: Defining high-level system boundaries and external entities.
  • Data Flow Diagrams (DFD): Modeling the movement of information through organizational processes.
  • Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD): Mapping database schemas to business logic.
  • System Analysis: Analyzing memory address labels and labeling logic (e.g., 95K and 135K address adjustments).
